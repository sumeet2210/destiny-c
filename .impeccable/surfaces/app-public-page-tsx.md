# Homepage redesign

- Scope: production route `/` only; visitor mode is Operate with a persuasive first viewport.
- Audience and job: a student quickly choosing a nearby place to eat, often while coordinating with friends.
- Primary actions: open search, choose a craving, take the existing quiz, inspect a current offer, or open a restaurant.
- Required evidence: live offer data, restaurant/menu data, upcoming event data, optional shared friend activity, and honest popularity sorting.
- Constraints: preserve all current queries, routes, authentication gates, analytics sources, save behavior, privacy rules, and conditional empty states. NIT Warangal remains context, not headline copy.
- Direction: the approved final prototype's light Destiny system—full wordmark, Soft Off-White canvas, black editorial fields, Destiny Teal actions, Manrope, cinematic food crops, and compact restaurant cards.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport turns the dinner debate into one search/shortlist decision beside a single food image.
- Responsive rule: full wordmark remains visible on mobile; controls stay at least 44px; cards remain compact; no horizontal page overflow at 320px.
- Migration boundary: other production routes retain the incumbent dark design until separately reviewed and converted.
